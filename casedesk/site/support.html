Email hello@tharamaya.com. Please don’t send copies of documents or receipt numbers — describe the problem instead. If something went wrong, Settings → Diagnostics → Share sends a log without document details.

Common questions

Do I need an account?No. Everything is saved on your phone. There is no sign-in.
How do I check a USCIS case?Open the case and tap “Copy number & open uscis.gov”. Paste the number on the USCIS page, tap Check Status, copy what it says, come back and tap “Paste what USCIS says”. The app never logs in to USCIS for you.
I’m getting a new phoneTurn on iCloud backup in Settings (iPhone), or use Settings → Back up to a file. On the new phone choose “Restore from iCloud” or “Restore from a backup” on the Welcome screen and type your backup password.
I forgot my backup passwordWe can’t recover it — backups are encrypted on your phone and we never see the password. Make a new backup with a new password from the phone that has your data.
I’m not getting remindersSettings → Notifications shows whether alerts are allowed. If they’re off, tap it to open iOS Settings and allow notifications.
A date looks wrongOpen the document and fix the field — status updates right away. Always check against the original document.
Where does the data come from?USCIS, the State Department, the Department of Labor and DHS. Each screen shows its date, whether it’s live or a built-in copy, and links the official source. Settings → About lists every source.
Are the document copies valid ID?No. Copies in the app are for your reference only — not an official ID.
