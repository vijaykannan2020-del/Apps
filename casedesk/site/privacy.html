Privacy Policy

Immigration Wallet · effective November 2026

Short version: your family’s documents and details stay on your phone. No ads, no analytics and no tracking. We never see your documents, names or receipt numbers. Two features are optional and send a little data to our server only when you use them: Community Q&A (if you sign in) and sharing your case timeline anonymously in Cases like mine.

What stays on your phone

Everything you addPeople, documents (photos and PDFs), dates, cases and receipt numbers, reminders and settings are saved only in the app’s storage on your device.
Reading documentsText is read from photos with Apple’s on-device text recognition (Google ML Kit on Android) and from PDFs on the phone. Nothing is uploaded to read it.
Reminders and alertsReminders before documents expire and alerts for case status changes are worked out and scheduled on your phone as local notifications.
Face IDOptional. iOS checks your face or passcode; the app only learns “unlocked” or not.
DiagnosticsA short log of what the app did (no document details) stays on the phone. It is only sent if you choose to share it with us.

Backups (optional)

Encrypted with your passwordBackups are encrypted on your phone (AES-256-GCM, key from your password with scrypt) before they leave the app. We don’t have your password and can’t open or recover a backup.
iCloud backupIf you turn it on, the encrypted file is saved to your own iCloud account (the app’s private iCloud storage). Apple stores it; we never receive it. The password is kept in your iPhone’s keychain so automatic backups can run.
Backup to a fileYou choose where the encrypted file goes (Files, a cloud drive, AirDrop). We never receive it.

What our server receives

The app downloads public information from our server: news and official updates, the Visa Bulletin, USCIS processing times, visa appointment wait times and guides. These requests contain nothing about you.

When you use a look-up tool, only what you type into that tool is sent: the job title, state and county for prevailing wages, or the employer name for H-1B employer data.

What to expect on a case asks the server for typical timing using only the form, the 3-letter office code from the receipt (EAC, WAC…), the received date, premium yes/no and visa class — never the receipt number.

Never sent: your documents, names, dates of birth, passport or A-numbers, receipt numbers, case statuses or anything else you saved.

Like any website, the server necessarily sees your device’s IP address to send the answer back. We don’t keep request logs, build profiles or share this with anyone.

Optional: Community Q&A

ReadingAnyone can read questions without signing in.
Signing inTo ask, answer or upvote you sign in with Apple. We keep only a one-way hash of Apple’s user id and a random session token (stored hashed). We never keep your email or real name; others see only a made-up display name you can change.
What we storeYour display name, the questions and answers you post, your upvotes, reports and blocks, and — only if you turn on “Notify me of answers” — a push token to tell you about new answers. Posts are public to app users. Posts with receipt numbers or other personal numbers are refused.
DeletingCommunity → Account → Delete account removes your account and everything you posted.

Optional: Cases like mine

If you turn on “Add my case anonymously” on a case, the app sends the form, 3-letter office code, received and decided dates, outcome, premium yes/no and visa class with a random id made on your phone. No receipt number, name or device id. Turning it off deletes the entry. Timelines are shown only in groups of at least 10.

Other services the app opens

USCIS case statusTo check a case, the app copies your receipt number and opens uscis.gov in Safari. You paste it there yourself; what you send to USCIS is covered by USCIS’s own privacy policy. The app never logs in to USCIS or reads the site for you.
Official websitesLinks to uscis.gov, travel.state.gov, dol.gov and news sources open in your browser under those sites’ policies.
App updatesThe app checks Expo’s update service for new versions; that request includes the app version and platform, nothing you saved.

What we don’t do

  • No account needed for anything except posting in Community.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • We don’t sell or share data — we don’t have it.

Your choices

Delete anything in the app at any time, or use Settings → Delete all data. Deleting the app removes everything on the phone. To remove iCloud backups, turn off iCloud backup in the app and delete the app’s data in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Immigration Wallet is not intended for children under 13 to use on their own. Parents may add children as family members; their details stay on the parent’s phone.
