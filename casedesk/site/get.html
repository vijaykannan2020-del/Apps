Immigration Wallet

Your family’s US immigration documents and dates, on your own phone.

Someone shared documents with you?

  1. Install the appUse a button below on your iPhone.
  2. Open the file in the appTap the .iwshare file you were sent and choose Immigration Wallet. Or open the app and tap I have a shared file (Settings → Import shared documents if the app is already set up).
  3. Enter the 6-word codeThe person who shared it tells you the code by phone or in person. It is never in the message or the file.

Private by design

Your documents stay on your phone. Shared files are locked with the code, and this page never sees the file or the code. No account, ads or tracking. Read the privacy policy.
